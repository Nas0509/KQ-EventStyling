Terms and Conditions

Please read these terms before submitting an event booking or ocular inspection request.

1. Accounts and information

Provide accurate contact and event information and keep your account credentials private. Update your profile if your contact details change so we can reach you about your request.

2. Services and package details

Package inclusions are described on the Services page. Crew meals and transportation are not included. Prices may vary by venue, and an ocular inspection is required to assess the event location and styling requirements. Any confirmed price or change will be communicated for your review.

3. Booking requests and dates

Submitting a booking is a request, not confirmation that KQ Event Styling has accepted the event. Requests are subject to schedule availability and review. Pending requests do not reserve a date or time. A booking is confirmed only after KQ approves the request and verifies the required payment.

4. Reservation fee and payment verification

A 30% reservation fee is required for a booking request. Pay the amount displayed using the payment QR code and enter the correct transaction or reference number. A transfer remains pending until KQ verifies it; submitting a reference number or making a transfer does not itself confirm a booking. The remaining balance is due after the event.

5. Venue travel expense

The system estimates travel expense from North Caloocan at ₱20 per kilometer and rounds the amount up to the next ₱100. The displayed distance is an estimate based on the selected city or municipality. Review the final amount shown in your request and contact KQ if the venue details are inaccurate.

6. Cancellation and rescheduling

Submit cancellation or rescheduling requests through your account. A request takes effect only after KQ reviews it and updates its status. The 30% reservation fee is non-refundable when a customer cancellation request is approved. Any other payment or balance arrangements will be communicated by KQ when the request is reviewed.

7. Ocular inspection requests

Ocular inspection requests depend on the selected location, date, time, and availability. The applicable inspection fee is shown in the request flow. The request is not confirmed until KQ reviews it and marks it approved. We will send a status notification to the email address on your account when available; please also check your account for the current status.

YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Notice

KQ Event Styling uses information you provide to operate your account and manage event services. This may include your name, email address, contact number, event and venue details, request history, and payment transaction reference.

How information is used

We use these details to review and manage bookings and ocular inspections, verify submitted payment references, contact you about your requests, and send service or status notifications. Information is stored in the online system used to operate the service and is available to authorized KQ personnel who manage requests.

Payment details

Do not submit passwords, card numbers, or other payment credentials in a transaction-reference field. KQ requests a transaction or reference number to help verify a transfer; payment is checked separately.

Questions or corrections

Contact KQ Event Styling at kqeventstyling@gmail.com if your account information needs correction or if you have a question about this notice.
